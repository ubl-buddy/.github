# UBL Buddy

**Open Peppol invoices like you open a PDF.**

UBL Buddy is a native desktop and mobile app that makes it effortless to open, view, and pay Peppol invoices (e-invoices, BIS Billing, UBL/XML files). Just double-click an invoice file and view it instantly — no account, no uploads, no hassle.

## Why UBL Buddy?

- **Native performance** — Lightning-fast, works offline
- **Privacy-first** — Your invoices stay on your device, never uploaded
- **Zero friction** — No account needed, install and go
- **Pay in seconds** — Scan the QR code and pay directly from your banking app

## Available on

- macOS (App Store)
- iPhone & iPad (App Store)
